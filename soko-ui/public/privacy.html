Soko Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026 · Applies to the Soko website at soko-app-eight.vercel.app and the Soko app on Android and Windows.

Soko is a local marketplace that connects retailers around Nairobi with customers for listings, ordering, delivery and chat. This policy explains what information Soko collects, why, and the choices you have. Soko is built and operated by an independent developer in Nairobi, Kenya, and is offered free of charge.

What we collect

Account details
Your name, phone number, a hashed password and, for retailers, your shop name and address.
Location
For retailers, the shop location you set. For customers, a delivery address you enter, and — only if you allow it in your browser — your approximate device location to show nearby shops.
Orders and chat
Items you order, delivery details, ratings you leave, and messages between you and a shop.
Photos
Product, shop and profile images you upload.
Technical data
Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) kept for security and debugging, and the settings you choose in the app (such as light or dark mode), which are stored on your own device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS inbox, files or photos unless you explicitly pick something to upload or paste.

How we use it

Only to provide the service described above: to run your account, show you your own data, let you share what you choose to share, keep the service secure, and fix problems. Payments are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout (for example M-PESA). Soko does not store card numbers or M-PESA PINs.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure (currently Vercel, Render and a managed PostgreSQL database) that may be located outside Kenya. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your profile and records inside the app.
  • Deletion — you can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Email gnm7208@gmail.com from the address or phone number on your account; deletion is completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply. Shared links you created stop working once your account is deleted.
  • Location and camera — permissions can be revoked at any time in your browser or device settings; the app keeps working without them.

Kenyan users have the rights set out in the Data Protection Act, 2019, including the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children

Soko is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the “last updated” date above will change and the app will point you to the new version.
